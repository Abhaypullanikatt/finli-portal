# Investment exploration consent

The Investment Explorer reuses the current financial profile, financial goal,
investment horizon, liquidity need, and educational risk assessment to explain
general investment categories.

The app stores the selected category interests, the profile and assessment
versions used, the explanation shown, and the content and rule versions. This
information is used only to provide, reproduce, export, correct, or delete the
educational result and to support an educational call requested by the user.

The explorer does not create an investment account, share information with an
investment provider, identify a product or security, place a transaction, or
promise a return.

Consent can be withdrawn from Privacy as easily as it is granted. Withdrawal
stops new personalized explorer results. Existing records remain available for
export and deletion under the displayed retention policy.
