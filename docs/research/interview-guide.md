# Target-user interview guide

## Recruitment

Recruit 30 Indian salaried participants who are roughly 20–30 years old, earn
₹20,000–₹1,00,000 monthly, and have limited confidence managing money. Include
different cities, income levels, living arrangements, debt exposure, dependants,
and investing experience.

Do not collect account numbers, passwords, PINs, identity documents, or exact
security holdings.

## Opening

Explain that the session evaluates a prototype, not the participant. Obtain
research consent and permission before recording. Run the session without
recording when permission is declined.

## Questions

1. Tell me what usually happens after your salary arrives.
2. What money decision currently feels most confusing?
3. How do you decide how much to save?
4. Have debt or unexpected expenses changed an investment plan?
5. What makes financial guidance feel trustworthy or untrustworthy?
6. Show the participant the onboarding prototype and ask them to think aloud.
7. Show the health explanation. What do they believe the number means?
8. Show the first roadmap action. Ask them to explain it in their own words.
9. Ask what evidence or explanation would make the action credible.
10. Test manual and voice-assisted expense entry and time both.
11. Ask what financial information they would refuse to enter and why.
12. Ask what they expect to happen when they withdraw consent or delete data.

Do not ask whether they “like” the product until after behavioral and
comprehension questions.

## Session outcomes

Record:

- Comprehended first action without help: yes/no
- Correctly explained rationale: yes/no
- Completed onboarding: yes/no and time
- Manual expense time
- Voice-assisted expense time
- Corrections required
- Trust concern
- Privacy concern
- Most valuable moment
- Would join a four-week pilot: yes/no

Proceed to the concierge pilot when at least 70% can explain their next action
and rationale without assistance and no high-severity safety issue is open.
