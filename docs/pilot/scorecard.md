# Four-week concierge pilot scorecard

## Cohort

- Target participants: 20
- Product scope: onboarding, adviser-reviewed health result and roadmap, manual
  and voice-assisted expenses, one contextual lesson, one monthly action, and
  one educational call
- Explicit exclusions: product recommendations, execution, bank connections,
  paid plans, and saved audio

## Weekly operating rhythm

### Before week one

- Confirm participant and research consent.
- Use only the approved pilot storage and retention procedure.
- Have the financial reviewer approve every manually generated result.
- Log incidents and corrections without storing unnecessary financial values in
  analytics.

### Weeks one to four

- Check whether the participant understands the current action.
- Review transaction corrections and time-to-entry.
- Deliver only the lesson connected to the current action.
- Record support minutes and exceptions.
- Ask whether circumstances changed before reusing a roadmap.

## Production gate

Proceed only when:

- At least 14 participants completed onboarding.
- At least 12 understood the first action and rationale.
- At least 10 remained active in week four.
- At least 8 completed the recommended monthly action.
- Median expense-entry time is below 15 seconds.
- No unresolved high-severity financial, privacy, security, or compliance issue
  remains.

If the gate fails, revise and repeat the pilot. Do not add unrelated features.
